Operating bias

Decision-first · not company doctrine

1 · Highest spend first

Pilot the accounts with the most monthly spend — not the “cleanest tracking” on a tiny budget. Low-spend accounts ($1.5–5K/mo as a feel) rarely produce enough attributable outcomes for value learning / Smart Bidding density.

  • Do: Prove the signal machine on big spenders first.
  • Low spend: Honest scope (brand + narrow test) — not the pilot for CallRail → value → graduate bidding.
  • Caveat: High spend with garbage primary conversions is still bad. Fix signal on the big accounts; don’t ignore quality.
  • Not policy: No hard dollar cutoff invented for FirmPilot — this is George’s first-pilot bias.

2 · CallRail is gravity

Connected call → AI draft score + confidence → human sample / uncertain review → CRM truth outranks AI → upload defensible lead/fee values to Google fast (not settlement theater).

  1. Connected / answered call lands
  2. AI scores + flags confidence
  3. Humans review uncertain / samples
  4. CRM disposition wins when it conflicts
  5. Qualified value → Google (dense, timely)

3 · Conversion ladder (roles)

  • Soft / diagnostic 60s or connected — density & diagnostics; not automatic primary
  • Stronger AI-qualified → human-qualified (primary candidates once proven)
  • North Star Retained / fee — measure always; often too sparse to bid alone

Torture question (“every 60s call a conversion?”): density feels necessary (George wants signal like 5–10/day when spend supports it) vs Google’s published evaluate-with-volume guidance. Prefer graded events + values over dumping every ring into Primary.

4 · Forms · bidding · expansion

  • Forms: Gate / score (light qualifying quiz). Raw form ≠ primary — bots spam.
  • Graduate bidding only after density + quality gates on a defensible primary (or value).
  • Expand after signal kicks: mid-intent → broad / AI Max → competitors → PMax / Display / YouTube.
  • Still build clean: search terms, ads, LPs, conversions, offline values — on the high-spend pilots.

Signal Ladder

Volume + quality · not depth alone
  1. Traffic
  2. Lead
  3. Legitimate Prospect
  4. Qualified Lead
  5. Consultation
  6. Retained Case

Bid toward the strongest reliable signal that still has enough volume on that account’s spend.

Why this matters
  • A call or form is not automatically valuable (junk, wrong practice, existing client, vendor…).
  • 60s / connected helps density and diagnostics; qualified + CRM-backed events should carry bidding.
  • Retained is the North Star but often too sparse in legal to be the only biddable event.
  • AI must not invent conversion values because a transcript “sounds good.”

Collapsed depth · cite before inventing
Google Ads · conversion volume (cited)

Pulled from Google Ads Help (Oct 2026 fetch). Legal PPC is sparse vs ecommerce — treat published numbers as evaluation / eligibility baselines, not a promise that retained-case bidding will work at low spend.

  • Smart Bidding (overview): You can turn strategies on without prior conversion data; Google recommends evaluating performance over periods with at least 30 conversions (e.g. a month or longer); 50 conversions called out for Target ROAS evaluation. Baselines vary by campaign type — check each strategy article. About Smart Bidding
  • Target CPA: Can start with no conversion history. For evaluation, Google recommends measuring the last 30 days including at least 30 conversions. About Target CPA
  • Target ROAS (Search / Shopping): At least 15 conversions in the past 30 days at the conversion-tracking level (other campaign types have different floors). Prefer reporting values for 4 weeks or 1–2 conversion cycles before activating value bidding. About Target ROAS
  • Value-based bidding (Search / Shopping): Same ≥15 conversions / 30 days note for Target ROAS; report two or more unique values; feed conversions as soon as available (daily offline uploads called optimal); shorter delays preferred. Upload values for 3 weeks or 1–2 conversion cycles before switching to value bidding. Value-based bidding
  • Display (stability guidance): If you care about consistently hitting a target over ~30 days, Google recommends driving at least 30 conversions per month per ad group; lower volume → higher CPA/ROAS fluctuation. Smart Bidding for Display
  • Maximize conversions: Requires conversion tracking; aims to spend the full daily budget. Strategy-specific volume floors are pointed from the Smart Bidding overview — do not invent a number if the live Max Conv article doesn’t state one for your campaign type. About Maximize conversions

Legal caveat: Retained cases and fee realizations are delayed and sparse. Google’s “15 / 30 / 50” figures describe platform learning and evaluation windows — they do not mean a $2K/mo PI account can safely bid only to retained. Prefer qualified intermediate signals with defensible values on high-spend pilots.
