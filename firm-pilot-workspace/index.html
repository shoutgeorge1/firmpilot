Signal Ladder

  1. Traffic
  2. Lead
  3. Legitimate Prospect
  4. Qualified Lead
  5. Consultation
  6. Retained Case

Google should optimize toward the strongest reliable signal that has enough volume.

Why this matters
  • A call or form is not automatically valuable (junk, wrong practice, existing client, vendor…).
  • Deeper verified outcome = stronger signal. Use the deepest event with enough volume.
  • Low budgets can’t support sparse retained-only optimization — pick the best signal that still has density.
  • AI must not invent conversion values because a transcript “sounds good.”

Meeting Mode

Listen first · pick one room
Executive Owner / leadership
  • Why paid in-house now — fill gaps, defend brand, replace outside PPC, or own acquisition?
  • What does a successful first quarter look like in your words?
  • How does paid pay for itself if there’s no separate PPC fee?
  • What does “7–8× return” mean — numerator, lag, paid-only vs blended?
  • Who decides budgets, conversion goals, bid strategy, engineering priority?
  • Can we pilot ~10 accounts before touching ~100?
Paid Media PPC / operators
  • What do you touch daily? Who owns strategy vs maintenance?
  • What conversion actions train bidding today?
  • Manual vs Smart Bidding / PMax / AI Max — where and why?
  • Biggest account messes you keep fighting?
  • Direction: QA, call review, search terms, intake feedback, anomalies — not reinventing strategy in every account.
  • Listen for: optimizing to every phone call · Max Clicks everywhere · $2K across 10 campaigns.
Engineering · AI Product / data / skills
  • What have you already automated? What is a “skill” here?
  • Ads API / developer token — read vs write for agents?
  • How are clients separated? Secrets, logging, sessions?
  • CRM + CallRail APIs/webhooks? Transcripts? Offline conversions today?
  • Lead statuses standardized? What can agents safely WRITE vs READ?
  • Frame: AI industrializes proven PPC logic — it doesn’t invent strategy.
