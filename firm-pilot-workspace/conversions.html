Signal ladder · what trains bidding vs what you measure

Rule

Bid toward the strongest reliable signal that still has enough volume.

Lead Call / form / chat land — density & diagnostics Soft
Legitimate prospect Not spam / wrong practice / existing-client noise Filter
Qualified lead Gravity for bidding once proven · CRM-backed preferred Stronger
Consultation Booked / held intake with real case potential Stronger
Retained North Star — measure always; often too sparse to bid alone North Star

Roles

  • 60s / connected = diagnostic / soft until proven — not auto Primary.
  • Qualified lead signal is gravity — vendor-agnostic (calls, forms, CRM).
  • CRM truth > AI — AI drafts; humans review uncertain; CRM wins.
  • Retained = North Star; often not enough volume alone.

Why this matters
  • A call or form is not automatically valuable (junk, wrong practice, existing client, vendor…).
