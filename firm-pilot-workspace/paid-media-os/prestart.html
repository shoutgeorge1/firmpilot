First-week questions

Ask before prescribingdiscover
  • What data is already centralized? What is the source of truth?
  • Which CRM systems / call platforms are supported?
  • How is GTM / WordPress managed? What internal APIs exist?
  • What existing AI skills exist? What does Product vs Engineering own?
  • Which clients are unhappy / urgent / highest spend / strongest economics?
  • How are retained cases tracked? What does the existing dashboard expose?
