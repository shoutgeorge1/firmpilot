Listen first. Let them describe the department and pain. Ask before prescribing. Don’t dump a 100-account agent pitch.

What I’m walking in to do

  1. 1Get access
  2. 2Baseline ~10 accounts
  3. 3Prove Google is learning truth or garbage
  4. 4Fix signals before structure theater
  5. 5Pilot, measure, then automate

Platform

FirmPilot intelligence about desired cases, markets, and site performance should guide every channel — with call/intake flowing back.

Paid’s job

Reinforce FirmPilot’s work — not a disconnected PPC agency. Biggest risk: conversion signal quality.

Economics

Show channel attribution and blended cost per qualified matter / retained case. A call ≠ valuable.

Who I’m talking to

Pick one audience. One panel at a time.

Owner / leadership

  • Why paid in-house now — fill gaps, defend brand, replace outside PPC, or own acquisition?Service economics and role scope.
  • What does a successful first quarter look like in your words?Avoid optimizing to vanity calls.
  • Reportedly no separate PPC fee / some firms have outside PPC — how does paid pay for itself?
  • What does “7–8× return” mean — numerator, lag, paid-only vs blended?
  • Who decides budgets, conversion goals, bid strategy, exceptions, engineering priority?
  • Can we pilot ~10 accounts before touching ~100?

AI engineering

  • What have you already automated? What is a “skill” here?
  • Ads API / developer token access level? Read vs write for agents?
  • How are clients separated? Secrets, logging, retained agent sessions?
  • CRM + CallRail APIs/webhooks? Transcripts? Anything uploading conversions to Google today?

Frame: AI industrializes proven PPC logic — not invents strategy.

OS → AI eng · AI / skills

PPC team

  • What do you touch daily? Who owns strategy vs maintenance?
  • What conversion actions train bidding today?
  • Manual vs Smart Bidding / PMax / AI Max — where and why?
  • Biggest account messes you keep fighting?

Direction: QA, call review, search terms, intake feedback, anomalies — not reinventing strategy in every account.

Cross-channel economics

Intake as budget drain

Human trust vs AI
