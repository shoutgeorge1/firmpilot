Thesis — share if the door opens

  • FirmPilot already develops intelligence about each firm’s desired cases, markets, competitors, demand, content, local presence, and site performance. That same intelligence should guide SEO, paid, social, local, pages, and follow-up — with call/intake outcomes flowing back to every channel.
  • Paid should reinforce and distribute FirmPilot’s work — not become a disconnected PPC agency. Biggest risk is often conversion signal quality, not campaign cosmetics.
  • Show direct channel attribution and blended cost per qualified new matter / retained case. Do not claim one channel created every case. A call or form is not automatically valuable.

Short deeper notes (meeting-safe)

Cross-channel & blended economics

SEO/content/social may create demand while outside PPC takes the branded click. Measure channel attribution and blended acquisition. Larger play: help the attorney govern the whole marketing budget with comparable case economics.

OS → Signal thesis
Intake as silent budget drain

Missed calls, slow follow-up, bad dispositions, shifting appetite waste every channel. A 30-minute existing-client call ≠ new matter. Duration is a filter.

OS → CallRail / calls
Human trust vs AI assembly

Attorneys need a credible human who explains the money and coordinates execution. AI assembles evidence. Relationship + transparent results = fortress — not proprietary lock-in.
