Meetings

Listen first · pick one room · ask before prescribing

Listen first. Let them describe the department and pain. Don’t dump a 100-account agent pitch.

Executive

Owner / leadership
  • Why paid in-house now — fill gaps, defend brand, replace outside PPC, or own acquisition?
  • What does a successful first quarter look like in your words?
  • Can we pilot best-candidate accounts before touching the long tail?
  • What does “7–8× return” mean — numerator, lag, paid-only vs blended?
  • Who decides budgets, conversion goals, bid strategy, engineering priority?
  • Reportedly no separate PPC fee / some firms have outside PPC — how does paid pay for itself?

Paid Media

PPC / operators
  • What conversion actions train bidding today — and are they Primary?
  • Call / form → qualified value path: how fast? how reliable?
  • Manual vs Smart Bidding / PMax / AI Max — where and why?
  • Biggest account messes you keep fighting?
  • Listen for: every phone call as primary · Max Clicks everywhere · thin budgets across many campaigns.
  • Who owns strategy vs day-to-day maintenance?

Engineering · AI

Product / data / skills
  • Call platform APIs/webhooks? Transcripts? Offline / Enhanced Conversions for Leads path today?
  • Ads API / developer token — read vs write for agents?
  • Lead statuses standardized? What can agents safely WRITE vs READ?
  • How are clients separated? Secrets, logging, retained agent sessions?
  • Frame: AI scores calls; humans approve uncertain; CRM wins conflicts.
  • What have you already automated? What is a “skill” here?
